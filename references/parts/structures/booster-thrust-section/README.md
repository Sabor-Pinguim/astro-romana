# Booster thrust section

The aft section of the Main Booster in the rocket builder. It is the hull the engines hang from and the tank stands on. It has **no engine and no tank of its own**: the crew picks an engine cluster and a tank from the catalog, and they attach at `Attach_Engines` and `Attach_Tank`. Real analogs: the Saturn V S-IC thrust structure (five F-1 engines) and the SLS core stage engine section (four RS-25).

**Why tier 1:** every rocket needs exactly one; it is not upgraded, only filled.

## 1. Game data

This is a normalized game module (Ø 1.00 m in the builder), not a real part, so its numbers are `game-balance`. The game reads them from `tools/modeling/parts/specs.mjs`. The engine and tank it carries bring their own real data (see `../../engines/` and `../../tanks/`).

## 4. Low-poly model

Generated by `tools/modeling/parts/structures.mjs` (`npm run models:build:parts -- booster-thrust-section`).

### 4.1 Shape in words

A short white skirt with a black band near the bottom, four thin black fins on the diagonals, a steel thrust plate closing the bottom and a small titanium hexagonal boss in the middle (where the center engine bolts on). An aluminum flange on top is the seat of the tank.

### 4.2 Node hierarchy

```text
BoosterThrustSection        origin: bottom of the thrust plate
├─ Attach_Top               (0, 0.5, 0)
├─ Attach_Bottom            (0, 0, 0)
├─ Attach_Tank              (0, 0.5, 0)   tank bottom interface
└─ Attach_Engines           (0, 0, 0)     cluster center; engines hang below
```

### 4.5 Simplify

- No engine, no tank, no plumbing: the parts attached to it show those.

## 6. Gaps and cautions

- All values are `game-balance`: the module is normalized and has no single real counterpart.
- The engine patterns place engines on rings; real clusters (Falcon 9 octaweb, Super Heavy) differ in spacing.
