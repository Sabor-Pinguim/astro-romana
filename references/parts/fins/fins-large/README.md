# Large fixed fins with fairings

Four large fixed fins with fairings at their roots, on the skirt of the Main Booster (`booster-thrust-section`). Fixed fins at the tail move the centre of pressure aft, which gives passive aerodynamic stability; larger fins give more. Real analog: the Saturn V first stage (S-IC) had four fins, each with a fairing that also covered an outer engine [1].

**Why tier 2:** the strongest damping of the liftoff gusts in the game, at a higher mass and cost than the small fins (tier 1).
