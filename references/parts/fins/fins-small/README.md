# Small fixed fins

Four small fixed fins bolted to the skirt of the Main Booster (`booster-thrust-section`). Fixed fins at the tail move the rocket's centre of pressure aft, so the rocket turns back into the airflow when a gust pushes it off course (passive aerodynamic stability). Real analog: the Redstone family (Mercury-Redstone, Jupiter-C) flew with four fins at the base [1].

**Why tier 1:** light and cheap, with a small effect; the large fins (tier 2) steady the ascent more but weigh and cost more.
